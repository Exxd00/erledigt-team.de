# Asset provenance

- `public/images/logo.jpg`: the user's supplied clean logo (attachment 5), unchanged.
- `public/images/hero-cleaning.webp`: generated specifically for this website with the built-in image
  generator. Art direction: a professional in navy workwear cleaning modern office glass with a pole
  from ground level; action on the right, quiet composition on the left for headings; natural daylight,
  realistic materials, blue brand palette, no text or invented branding.
- `public/images/solar-cleaning.webp`: generated specifically for this website. Art direction: close
  detail of a gentle water-fed cleaning brush on ground-mounted solar modules, realistic reflections,
  careful equipment use, blue palette, no text or invented logos.

Both generated originals are 1536 × 1024 pixels and were encoded as WebP for delivery. They are
illustrative scenes, not claimed customer projects or photographs of the actual team. No external
stock photography was imported. Icons are the installed Lucide React package; the typeface is the
locally installed Manrope package.

The October 8 refresh adds five 1536 × 1024 generated illustrations: office, floor, textile,
stairs and exterior cleaning. Built-in image generation was used; PNG originals were encoded
as WebP without editing their contents. Exact prompts and saved paths are in
`docs/generated-assets.json`. All five are uploaded to ImgBB and have matching local fallbacks
in `public/images`. Illustrations are labelled and do not imply photographs of real customer work.
